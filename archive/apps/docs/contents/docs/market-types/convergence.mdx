title: Convergence
---

**Question:** Are two assets **closer together** or **further apart** at the end vs at lock?

You are betting on the **relationship** between two feeds, not on either asset in isolation.

## How it settles

**Lock** records the spread (or ratio). **Settlement** reads both feeds again. If the gap **narrowed**, **Converge** wins; if it **widened**, **Diverge** wins. If the change is **within a defined tolerance**, the market may **void** and refund.

## When to use it

Relative-value views—e.g. whether ETH **outperforms** BTC **this period** expressed as spread movement.
