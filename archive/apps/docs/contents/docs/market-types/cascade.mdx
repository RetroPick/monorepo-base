title: Cascade
---

**Question:** How many **stacked** support or resistance **levels** were **broken** during the round?

You pick a **tier** for “how many breaks.” **More breaks** can map to **higher** tiers with different payoffs—similar spirit to ladder weighting.

## How it settles

Using **extreme** price through the epoch (e.g. **high** for upward stacks), the contract counts how many defined levels were **broken** and selects the matching tier.

## When to use it

Trend or **breakout** strength—when you expect a run through multiple **obvious** levels, not just a quiet close.

### Technical note

Cascade shares the **OHLC / path** DNA with corridor-style resolution in the engineering doc; read `currentSmartContract.md` for feed and reporting details.
