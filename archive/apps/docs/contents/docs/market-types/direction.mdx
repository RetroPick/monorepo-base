**Question:** Will this asset finish **higher or lower** than a **reference price** taken at lock?

## How it settles

At **lock**, a price snapshot is stored. At the **end** of the round, the feed is read again. **Up** wins if the final price is **above** the lock price; **Down** wins if **below**. If the two readings are **exactly equal**, the market **voids** and deposits are refunded.

## Example

- Lock: ETH **$2,500** · Close: **$2,680** → **Up** wins.

## When to use it

You have a simple short-term directional view and want a two-outcome market without extra thresholds or zones.
