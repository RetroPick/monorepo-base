## Plain-language walkthrough

1. **Choose a market** — Read the headline and the resolution text. Make sure you know what “winning” means.
2. **Pick a side** — Often two outcomes; some templates have more buckets or tiers.
3. **Add funds** — Your stake joins the pool for that outcome. Prices can reflect what the crowd believes; they are not a guarantee.
4. **Wait for lock** — After lock, the rules are fixed.
5. **Settlement** — The protocol reads feeds and applies the rule you saw up front.
6. **Payout or void** — Winners receive from the combined pools; voids return stakes per the rules. You may need a **claim** step depending on the product version.

## Who pays whom

Generally, **losers’ stakes fund winners** (after fees). Your share depends on how much you put in and how the contract splits the pool for that template—especially in weighted tiers (e.g. ladder markets).

### Technical note

Positions are represented on-chain; settlement may involve resolver modules, conditional tokens, and explicit `claim` calls. See `currentSmartContract.md` in the repo for exact mechanics.
