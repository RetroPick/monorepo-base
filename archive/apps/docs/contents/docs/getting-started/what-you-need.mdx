## Everyone

- A **clear question** on each market: what is being measured, and when does the round end?
- Read the **resolution rules** on the market page before you put money in.
- Understand you can **lose your stake** if your side does not win (except in a **void**, where rules may return deposits).

## Crypto essentials (short)

- A **wallet** app that supports the chain RetroPick runs on for your deployment.
- **Stablecoins** (often USDC) are commonly used to enter pools—subject to what the app supports.
- Small amounts of the chain’s **native token** pay **transaction fees (gas)** when you approve, deposit, claim, etc.

### Technical note

Exact tokens, chains, and UI flows can change by deployment. Follow the in-app prompts and the official RetroPick interfaces only.
