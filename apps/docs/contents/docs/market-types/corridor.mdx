title: Corridor
---

**Question:** Did price **stay inside** an upper/lower band for the **entire** round?

Unlike “close in a bucket,” corridor cares about **intraday** extremes—if price **tags** either boundary, that can decide the outcome.

## How it settles

**OHLC-style** extremes (high/low across the epoch) are compared to the band. **Inside** wins if **both** high and low respected the corridor; **Outside** wins if either boundary was touched or crossed (per template).

## When to use it

Range-bound **behavior** over time—not just where the candle **closes**.

### Technical note

Corridor often relies on **TrustedReporter** / event-oracle **OHLC** paths feeding resolved highs and lows—see the contract reference.
