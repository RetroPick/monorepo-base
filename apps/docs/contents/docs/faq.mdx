## Is this financial advice?

No. These docs explain **how the mechanism works**, not what you should buy or sell. Prediction markets are **risky**; you can lose your entire stake.

## What if the UI or a link looks wrong?

- Use only **official** RetroPick interfaces.
- **Verify** contract addresses for your network using authoritative deployment info.
- Never paste your **seed phrase** anywhere.

## Testnet vs mainnet

Deployments on **testnets** use tokens with **no real-world value**. Treat **mainnet** as real money at risk.

## Who do I contact for bugs?

Follow your deployment’s official support channels (Discord, email, or issue tracker). This documentation site may lag the latest release—**on-chain rules and the market page text** are the source of truth for a given market.
