## In simple terms

RetroPick markets do not rely on a person typing in the winning price. At settlement, the protocol reads from **authorized data feeds**—the same family of **Chainlink** infrastructure used widely in DeFi—so the number is consistent, timestamped, and expensive to fake at scale.

## What this means for you

- The **resolution criteria** are fixed after **lock**.
- The **data source** is known up front (shown in product and in contract parameters for a template).
- The outcome is **computed** from those readings using the rule printed on the market—**not** by moderator vote.

## Edge cases

Feeds can misbehave; templates define **void** conditions for ties or invalid combinations of readings. When in doubt, read the market’s own resolution copy.

### Technical note

RetroPick supports multiple Chainlink **oracle classes** (e.g. price, rate, SmartData, macro) and **TrustedReporter** paths where configured. The Solidity reference (`currentSmartContract.md`) documents checkpoints **A/B**, multi-feed **Composite** reads, and **OHLC** paths for corridor/cascade-style markets.
