## For non-crypto readers

Think of a wallet as your **account** for on-chain markets: it holds the keys that prove you own positions and can withdraw winnings. RetroPick does not “log you in” with a password alone—you connect a wallet the same way many DeFi apps do.

## Connecting a wallet

1. Install a supported wallet (browser extension or mobile).
2. Open the RetroPick app and choose **Connect**.
3. Confirm the connection request in your wallet.

## Network

Make sure your wallet is on the **same network** the app expects (e.g. a testnet or mainnet deployment). If the network is wrong, transactions will fail or go to the wrong chain.

## Approvals and gas

When you trade with ERC-20 tokens, you may need to **approve** the protocol contract to move a limited amount of your tokens. Each transaction consumes **gas** paid in the chain’s native asset.

### Technical note

Use block explorers to verify contract addresses and transactions. Never share **seed phrases** or **private keys** with anyone or any website.
