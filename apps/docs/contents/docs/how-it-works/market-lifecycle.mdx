---
title: Market lifecycle
---

Every RetroPick market follows the **same basic story**. Only the **question** and **how oracle data is combined** change between market types.

1. A market **opens** with a defined question and settlement conditions.
2. You **deposit** into the outcome you believe will happen.
3. At a specific time, the system **locks**: rules stop changing.
4. At settlement, **authorized market data** is read automatically.
5. The **result is calculated** and **winners** are paid from the pool according to the rules.
6. **No one** can change the outcome after the market has **locked**.

## Voids (refunds)

Sometimes the rules say there is **no fair winner**—for example an exact tie on a boundary in a direction market, or a tolerance band in a relative-value market. Then the market may **void**: stakes are returned instead of paying one side the whole pot.

See **Market types** for per-template void behavior.

## Technical note

On-chain, a **template** defines the market type, feeds, and parameters. An **epoch** (or “round”) moves through open → lock → resolve → claim phases. The implementation uses Chainlink-family feeds and audited contracts; see **Technical overview**.
