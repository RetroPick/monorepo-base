## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

Certain features of the Services may invite or require you to provide information. You are not obligated to provide this information, but doing so may be necessary to access certain features. The information you may provide includes:

**Contact information** such as your name and email address, for example when you contact us for support or sign up for notifications.

**Communication information** including the content of messages you send us through the Services or other channels such as email or social media.

**Marketing preferences** such as whether you wish to receive communications from us about the Services.

**Feedback and suggestions** you share with us voluntarily.

### Wallet and On-Chain Information

When you connect a self-hosted wallet to the Protocol, we may receive your public wallet address. We do not receive, store, or have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or any credentials that give access to your funds.

When you interact with the Protocol by depositing into a market, switching positions, claiming payouts, or performing any other on-chain action, that interaction is recorded permanently on the public blockchain. Our indexing infrastructure reads this publicly available data to power Protocol features including your position history and claimable amounts.

### Information Collected Automatically

We and our service providers automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Services:

**Device and browser information** including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution, and language settings.

**Usage information** including the pages or screens you view, how long you spend on each, navigation paths, access times, and referring URLs.

**Cookie and tracking data** as described in the Cookies section below.

## Blockchain and Public Data

Transactions you execute through the Protocol are recorded on Base L2, a public blockchain. This means your wallet address and all transaction details are permanently and publicly visible to anyone who queries the blockchain. We cannot delete or alter your on-chain transaction history. Information recorded on-chain exists on a decentralised public network outside our control.

Our indexing services read this public data to operate the Protocol. The public nature of blockchain data does not affect our obligations regarding any additional personal information you provide through our Interfaces.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the Services:

**Session cookies** expire when you close your browser and help maintain your session.

**Persistent cookies** remain on your device for a set period and remember your preferences.

**Analytics cookies** help us understand how users interact with the Services so we can improve them.

**Third-party cookies** may be placed by analytics or other service providers we use.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the Services function. We do not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals.
